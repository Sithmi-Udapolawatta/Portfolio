About me

A designer who thinks like a user

Hi, I'm Sithmi — a UI/UX Designer with 3+ years of experience designing intuitive digital products for startups and growing businesses across web and mobile platforms.

I focus on transforming complex ideas into seamless, user-centered experiences by balancing user needs with business goals. My design approach begins with empathy and research, and evolves into clean, functional interfaces that feel natural and engaging.

Over the years, I've worked on products across industries including healthcare, e-learning, tourism & travel, ERP, and innovation management for both local and international clients.

Beyond design execution, I've also supported the training of interns by guiding them through UI/UX design processes, workflows, and industry-standard practices.

My Design Process

From idea to impact

01

Discovery

Go through the client's requirements closely to understand what the project needs to achieve.

02

Research

Research the project domain and industry so decisions are grounded in real context.

03

Ideation

Work out the system flow and sketch early ideas on paper before touching a screen.

04

Wireframing

Translate sketches into low-fidelity layouts in tools like Figma, shaping structure first.

05

AI-Assisted Exploration

Bring AI tools into the process to explore extra directions and speed up iteration.

06

Design Critique

Share the work with other team members and gather their perspectives on it.

07

Iteration

Fold that feedback and any new ideas back into the designs until the flow feels right.

08

Prototyping

Bring the designs to life as an interactive prototype, ready to test and hand off.
